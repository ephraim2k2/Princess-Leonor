Biography

About Princess Leonor

Her Royal Highness Leonor de Borbón y Ortiz, Princess of Asturias, is the heir presumptive to the throne of Spain as the elder daughter of King Felipe VI and Queen Letizia.

Heir Presumptive to the Crown of Spain

Born on October 31, 2005 in Madrid, Princess Leonor represents the modern future of Spain's monarchy. As Princess of Asturias, Girona, and Viana, she undertakes official duties with exceptional grace, leadership, and commitment to global public service.
